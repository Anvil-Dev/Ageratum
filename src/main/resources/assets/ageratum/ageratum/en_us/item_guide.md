# Iron Ingot Uses

<item id="minecraft:iron_ingot"/>

Iron ingots are one of the most versatile materials in Minecraft. This guide explains their primary uses and crafting recipes.

## Basic Uses

Iron ingots can be used to craft:
- **Iron Tools** - Pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes, swords
- **Iron Armor** - Full armor sets for protection
- **Buckets** - For carrying liquids
- **Cauldrons** - For potion brewing
- **Doors** - Iron doors for secure areas
- **Hoppers** - Item sorting and collection

## Advanced Uses

### Iron Blocks

<recipe id="minecraft:iron_block"/>

Craft 9 iron ingots together to make an iron block, useful for storage and building.

### Pistons

<recipe id="minecraft:piston"/>

Combine iron ingots with wood and redstone to create pistons for redstone machines.

### Rails

<recipe id="minecraft:rail"/>

Mix iron ingots with stpicks to create various types of rails for minecarts.

## Smelting Tips

- Mine iron ore with a stone pickaxe or better
- Smelt iron ore in a furnace with coal or wood
- Each ore produces 1 iron ingot
